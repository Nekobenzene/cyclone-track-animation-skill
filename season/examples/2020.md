# 2020 — Western North Pacific

The season's own quirks. Prose, deliberately: a note here is something a human
reads before rendering, not a config anything parses. If a note turns into
something the renderer must **always** do, it belongs in
`references/season-workflow.md` or `references/season-style-spec.md` instead.

## Shape of the season

26 systems, 642 fixes, 8 May to 25 Dec. Nothing at all in January to April, so
at 15 days/s the first four months of the screen time pass in a few seconds and
the first storm appears about 9 s in. That is expected, not a bug.

Two dense stretches matter for pacing:

* **late August** — Bavi, Maysak, Haishen in sequence, and the three strongest
  of that group carry most of the season's ACE
* **mid-October to mid-November** — Saudel, Molave, Goni, Atsani, Etau, Vamco,
  the busiest block of the year, six systems on the map at once at its peak

Goni is the season's headline: peak 170 kt / 884 hPa on 31 Oct 18Z, and four
landfalls in the Philippines inside 24 hours at 151 / 134 / 93 / 60 kt. That
sequence is the best single test of the landfall size gradient.

## Systems with no name

Four never got one, so `Num.txt` line 3 is `-` and there is no naming stamp to
find:

| storm | peak | note |
|---|---|---|
| `WP06_Six` | 45 kt | JTWC only, no disturbance phase |
| `WP12_Twelve` | 25 kt | only 9 fixes, 2 days long |
| `WP20_Twenty` | 30 kt | |
| `WP26_Krovanh` | 30 kt | the season's last system, late December |

The label falls back to the JTWC number for these. `WP06_Six` and `WP23_Atsani`
also have no disturbance segment at all — their tracks start in `TC.txt`, which
the phase derivation handles because boundaries are derived, never hard-coded.

## Systems with no landfall and no damage

Six have a zero-byte `landfall.txt` and a zero-byte `damage.txt`. The empty
placeholder is skipped rather than contributing a zero point:

`WP06_Six`, `WP12_Twelve`, `WP14_Dolphin`, `WP15_Kujira`, `WP16_ChanHom`,
`WP20_Twenty`.

`WP16_ChanHom` is the notable one — 80 kt at peak and still no landfall; it
recurved and stayed at sea.

## Data shape notes

* Every storm sits entirely west of the dateline, so no track crosses it. The
  season's longitude span is 97.0 to 166.5 E.
* `WP07_Mekkhala/TC` and `WP09_Bavi/TC` each carry two 3-hourly steps among
  otherwise 6-hourly fixes. The interpolation reads the real fix times, so this
  needs no special handling — but do not assume a uniform step.
* Six fixes sit outside the usual wind/pressure family. Checked and left alone,
  they are real meteorology, not typos:
  * `WP22_Goni` 170 kt / 884 hPa — the deepest of the year, and off the family
    curve in the correct direction
  * `WP10_Maysak` and `WP11_Haishen` in their `EX` phase, where the tropical
    wind/pressure relation no longer holds
  * `WP23_Atsani` in its last three fixes, same reason
* `WP01_Vongfong` has 7 landfalls; `WP02_Nuri` through the Philippines is a
  single one.

## Basin

The basin rule picks **WPAC** with a coverage ratio of 0.5436, next best AUS at
0.2051. The resulting window is `90..190 E, -10..70 N`, which is exactly the
hand-written `wpac` preset this season used before the rule existed — a useful
check that the rule still lands where it should.

Every storm is inside WPAC, so the whole season shares one sheet and one window.

## Sound

The landfall sounds must be real `.ogg` files in `SMCY1.3/landfall/`. They have
been shortcuts to a drive that no longer exists at least once, which fails the
sound stage only after the whole video has rendered. Check that directory before
starting a long run.

## The cards (stage 6), for this season

The numbers the card layer arrives at, as a check that a re-run is still right:

| read-out | at the rate peak, 2020-10-31 18:00 | at the season's end |
|---|---|---|
| ACE rate | **11.56**/day — the season's maximum, at Goni's own 170 kt fix | 0 |
| cumulative ACE | 129.4 | **153.3** |
| deaths | 242 | 399 |
| loss | 209 亿元 | 265 亿元 |
| tally C5 / MH / HU / TS / TD | 1 / 6 / 11 / 19 / 22 | 1 / 7 / 12 / 23 / 26 |

* **The rate is real time** — `4/1e4 x SUM v^2` over the systems on the field,
  gated at 34 kt like the cumulative curve — and not the 24 h difference it used to
  be. A difference lags the events by half a day and peaks early: it put the
  season's maximum at 10.73 on 10-31 **06:00**, while Goni was still
  intensifying. The real-time rate peaks when the season is deepest, at **11.56**
  on 10-31 **18:00**, Goni's own 170 kt fix, on screen **232.623 s** (frame 27915
  at 120 fps). The fastest fall is **−0.920/day per hour** at 2020-11-01 00:00,
  screen 233.123 s — Goni's collapse, which a 24 h average could not show either.
  Only Goni contributes to any of it.
* At the peak instant the ungated sum would read 11.92: Atsani is on the field at
  30 kt and adds 0.36. The 34 kt gate is the reason it does not, and the reason
  the rate integrates to 151.5 against the 153.3 the card books.
* The tally's totals are cumulative up the stack; the season ends at
  1/7/12/23/26 against a bar axis of 30, i.e. 87 % full.
* **No count reverses this season.** All 26 systems were numbered, every one that
  reached HU was named, and every one that was named was numbered, so the five
  rulers nest. The non-nesting case in `season-cards.md` §5 has no example here,
  which means that code path is unexercised by real data.
* The 3 TD-only systems are exactly the three the definition predicts — 06W, 12W
  and 20W, numbered and never named. `WP26_Krovanh` is *not* one of them: it was
  named even though it peaked at 30 kt, so it counts as TS.

## The summary banners (stage 6)

26 banners, one per system, entering at each dissipation. At most **3** are ever on
screen at once: Jangmi / Mekkhala / Six, around screen 83.8 s, is the only
three-deep moment of the season - and the only place the 0.1 s top-to-bottom
stagger is visible, when Jangmi's departure lifts the other two. The art spreads
over TD x3, TS x11, C1 x5, C3 x3, C4 x3 and C5 x1 - Goni, the only C5.

`WP26_Krovanh` dissipates 285.99 s into a 288.84 s film, so its banner is still on
screen when the film ends and never runs its exit. That is the layer refusing to
re-time its base, not a bug.

### The ACE ruler's art changed after the fact

The plate master was rendered with the original `ace_color_1000.png`. That file is
shared with pass 1, and it has since been replaced with a more saturated strip
that reads better on the dark panel. **A plate rendered before that replacement
and cards rendered after it show two different ACE ramps in the same film.** Either
re-render the plate, or accept the mismatch knowingly.
